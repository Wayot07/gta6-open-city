**Solmar City** is a top-down 2D open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. You are Rex, a wheelman for fixer Dante, loose in a sun-soaked coastal city of 3,200 × 2,400 streets — a beach, a park, a port, traffic and pedestrians everywhere. Steal cars (the driver bails and runs), out-drive police cars that chase and ram you, dodge foot cops who can bust you, and work through 4 story missions from a quiet pickup to robbing the Solmar Trust vault at 3 wanted stars. Wanted level 0–5 stars, combat with pistol and fists, health pickups, a day/night cycle with headlights after dark, synthesized WebAudio sound (engine hum, sirens, gunshots, a bassline loop), particles, screen shake, a HUD with minimap, and localStorage saves. Controls: WASD/arrows move, E enter/steal a car, mouse aim + click to shoot, F punch, Space sprint/handbrake, P pause, M mute, H help.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
